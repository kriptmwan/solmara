Solmara is a 2D top-down open-world crime game set in a fictional sun-soaked coastal city. Wander the streets on foot or grab a car — any car you can get into is yours to drive. Traffic flows around you, pedestrians cross the sidewalks, and every crime you commit pushes your wanted level up, from 0 to 5 stars, with police closing in to take you down. Get caught and it's BUSTED; get taken out and it's WASTED.

There is a 5-mission story chain to work through, plus side work between jobs: pick up taxi fares around the city and hunt down hidden packages tucked away in corners most drivers never look. A HUD with a live minimap keeps you oriented, synthesized sound effects (WebAudio, no audio files) bring the city to life, and your progress saves to localStorage so you can pick up where you left off.

Everything runs on plain ES modules under `game/` — no libraries, no frameworks, no network calls. Open the files and the whole game is right there.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Play with keyboard and mouse.
